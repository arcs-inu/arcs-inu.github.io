Brief Bio

Geunhaeng Lee received a B.S. degree in electronic & electrical engineering from Dankook University, Kyung-Ki, Republic of Korea (ROK), in 2014, and a Ph.D. degree in electrical and electronic engineering from Yonsei University, Seoul, ROK, in 2021, respectively.

He was with System LSI Business, Samsung Electronics, Hwaseong, ROK from 2021 to 2023 as a Staff Engineer, where he was involved with design of UWB RF transceiver (IEEE 802.15.4z). From 2023 to 2025, he was with the dept. of Electronic Engineering, Andong National University, Kyungpook, ROK. Since March 2025, he has been with the dept. of Information and Telecommunication Engineering, Incheon National University, Incheon, ROK as an assistant professor.

His research interests are RF amplifiers including PA and LNA, mixed-signal circuits, and circuit & system for wireless communication. He is also interested in research on UWB transceiver systems, mm-wave ICs and digital transmitter.

Dr. Lee received a bronze prize of the circuit design division at the 27th Humantech Paper Award hosted by Samsung Electronics in 2021.

He also serves as a technical reviewer for IEEE SSCL, IEEE TCAS-II, IEEE TBioCAS and IEEE JSSC.

Research Overview
1. Wireless Transceiver IC design (Communication)

And these days, UWB application is the most important key idea for various application. UWB technique is applied to any smart devices for communication and precise ranging simultaneously. And, also as we know the mm-wave IC is applied to any automotive radar application or 5G system.

1-a. High-data-rate (>Gbps) Fully Integrated Transceiver IC with advanced modulation technique

  • Extended – Multi Pulse Position Modulation (E-MPPM) (IEEE ISSCC 2021 & IEEE JSSC 2022)
  • Amplitude – Time Shift Keying (ATSK) Transceiver IC System for Brain Computing Interfaces (IEEE TBioCaS 2024)

1-b. IEEE 802.15.4z Standard Transceiver (Designed from Samsung) for Precise Ranging and Communication Simultaneous and Child Presence Detection

2. Wireless Sensor (Range, Angle) IC design

2-a. A Time Domain Artificial Intelligence Radar System Using 33-GHz Direct Sampling for Hand Gesture Recognition

The convergence of AI and RF leads to the expansion of new boundaries and a paradigm shift.

2-b. Angle of Arrival wireless sensor for localization.

2-c. Position Tracking using the time difference of arrival (TDOA) using a receiver array

3. RF/Analog IC Design

3-a. Wideband LNA using Active Inductor for multi-standard

3-b. High-speed Active Sampler using post-distortion for Linearization
